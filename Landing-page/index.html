Why GenHub

Solving 20 Years of Modding Friction

Zero Hour was never built for multi-mod setups, Steam overlays, or modern operating systems. GenHub isolates your files so you can install, test, and switch setups cleanly.

The Classic Car Analogy • Community Context

“Think of Zero Hour like a classic car we all love, like a blue W203 Mercedes-Benz. Over the years we have upgraded it with custom dashboards (ControlBar), better steering (HotKeys), extra performance (GenTool), and sent it to the shop for servicing and stage 1 tuning (GenPatcher).

The problem is that switching between a modded setup and a competitive setup usually means reinstalling the game or juggling folders. For Steam players, installing mods traditionally requires overwriting files in your clean game directory. That breaks playtime tracking, disables the Steam Overlay, and risks getting wiped whenever Steam runs a file check. Managing 1,200 maps gets tedious, and digging through your documents folder to install or share replays is a pain.”

Manual Installation

  • Overwritten Game Files: Installing a mod copies files directly into your game directory, requiring manual backups or a complete reinstall when you want to switch back to vanilla.
  • Fragile Steam Setups: Copying mod files directly into your Steam folder leaves them vulnerable to being wiped whenever Steam verifies game files, and you can only run one mod setup at a time.
  • Third-Party Network Clutter: Playing online with friends often requires running third-party tools like GameRanger or Radmin to connect.
  • Scattered Maps and Replays: Managing custom maps and sharing match replays means digging through obscure folders in Documents and guessing file versions.

With GeneralsHub

  • Isolated Mod Profiles: Each mod setup runs from its own profile using hardlinks. Your original game installation stays untouched, and switching takes one click.
  • Clean Steam Integration: Profiles launch through Steam with playtime tracking and overlay support intact, without polluting your main game directory or risking verification wipes.
  • Direct Online Multiplayer: Generals Online provides built-in matchmaking and peer-to-peer connections without third-party network tools like GameRanger or Radmin.
  • One-Click Maps and Replays: Drag and drop map folders to install them instantly with map previews, and open matching tournament replays directly.
